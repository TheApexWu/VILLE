# VILLE is not

A list of refusals. Equal billing with the README. Read first.

---

## VILLE is not a map.
A map is a totalizing claim about a territory. A `cityPack` is a partial, dated, signed collection of fragments. It will never claim to be *the* city. There will be many packs, including incompatible ones. Coverage is not a goal. Gaps are honest.

## VILLE is not a search engine.
There is no global query box. You cannot ask "find me coffee shops." You browse by where you are, or where a fragment points. Ranking is not a feature; it is the thing being refused. If you want search, use the city — walk.

## VILLE is not navigation.
No routing. No turn-by-turn. No ETA. No "fastest route." The device does not know where you are going and does not want to. Movement through the city is the user's business, not the device's.

## VILLE is not social.
No profiles. No followers. No likes. No comments. No activity feed. No nearby-users. A fragment carries one author's signature, not a thread. If you want a conversation, have one in person.

## VILLE is not AI for cities.
The runtime has no model. The beacon does not infer, recommend, summarize, or generate. AI lives in the build pipeline — translating sources, cleaning OCR, reconciling IDs. By the time content reaches the device, it has been chosen by a human and stamped with provenance.

## VILLE is not a personal-data product.
The format has no `user_id`, no `favorite`, no `visited`, no `pinned`, no `history`. There is no private layer. If you cannot share it under a license, the format cannot hold it. Your relationship to a place is yours, not the device's.

## VILLE is not complete.
A pack is partial by design. Some buildings, communities, practices, and lives are simply not in the pack — sometimes because the data does not exist, sometimes because the community has not consented, sometimes because the right answer is silence. Opacity is a feature, not a bug. The right not to be mapped is real.

## VILLE is not a platform.
No SDK. No app store. No marketplace. No revenue share. No analytics. The format is CC0; the firmware is MIT; the packs carry their own licenses. There is nothing to buy except, eventually, a device — and only if you want one. You can build your own.

## VILLE is not a startup.
One steward. No team. No cap table. No roadmap quarter. No growth target. No DAU. Quarterly ritual is one new pack or one improvement to the spec. If the steward loses interest, the format and the published packs persist. The funeral plan is the day-one plan.

## VILLE is not for everyone.
It is for people who already love a city enough to sit with it slowly. It will not convert anyone. It will not scale. It will not win. That is the shape of the thing.

---

*If a proposed feature contradicts one of the above, the feature is wrong, not the refusal. If a refusal turns out to be wrong, change it here first — in writing, with a date — before changing the code.*
